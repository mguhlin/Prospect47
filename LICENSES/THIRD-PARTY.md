# Third-party inventory

No third-party runtime libraries or production assets are included in the initial browser prototype.

Luanti has not yet been vendored. The Phase 0B candidate is the MIT-licensed `paradust7/luanti-wasm` wrapper pinned at `69e94f3a43422f10f07a4e0630ff413be1354669`. That wrapper currently pins its own Luanti fork and several dependencies. Their complete notices must be copied into the release distribution before any built artifact ships.

The three root repository PNG files are treated as project concept art pending confirmation of their creator, generation method, and selected distribution license. Do not redistribute them as production assets until that information is recorded.

All in-game textures under `game/mods/*/textures/` are original Prospect 47 assets licensed CC BY 4.0. See `LICENSES/PROSPECT47-ASSETS.txt` for the full inventory.
